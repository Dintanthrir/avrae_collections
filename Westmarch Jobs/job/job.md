```
!job <skill/tool> [-phrase "<description>"]
```

`!job` allows your character to earn income using the skill or tool of their choice as a downtime activity.

**Tools**
Proficiency in a tool is not required to use it in a job. In order to include your proficiency bonus in the roll you must add the tool to the list of your tools with proficiency/expertise using `!tool`.

_Example:_ `!tool pro thieves`

**Resolution**
Earnings are calculated based on your skill/tool check roll and automatically added to your coin purse. See `!help game coins`
